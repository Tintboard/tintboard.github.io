Tintboard for Jira

Your admin chooses label, status, column, swimlane and card colours once, for the whole site or one project, and adds the fields every card should show. Everyone sees them on boards, the backlog, list view, search results and the issue view, on company- and team-managed projects.

Join the betaGet the extension

Needs the free Tintboard browser extension for Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Without it, people still see the colour legend in each issue.

Label colours

Any label, from a 12-colour accessible palette or your own hex colour. Text colour is chosen for readable contrast.

Status colours

A colour per workflow status, beyond Jira's grey, blue and green, in the issue view, backlog, list and search.

Column colours

A coloured bar and tint on each board column's title, or reuse your status colours in one click.

Card colour rules

Label, status, priority, issue type, component, epic or parent, summary text, assignee, "assigned to me", overdue, due within N days or not updated for N days. First match wins; tint or stripe; one board or all.

Work-in-progress limits

Set a limit per column: its title turns red when it holds more cards.

Ready-made schemes

Scrum basics, Support team or Bugs first: a coloured board in two minutes. Includes a colour-blind-safe palette.

Fields on cards

Up to 3 extra fields at the bottom of each board card: story points, sprint, components or any custom field. Team-managed boards can't do this in Jira.

Colours per project

Give a project its own labels, statuses, columns, swimlanes and card rules, on top of the colours for all projects. Boards and searches that mix projects stay right.

Swimlane colours

Group a board by assignee, epic or anything else, and each swimlane gets its colour, matched by its title.

Issue view comfort

A narrow details panel, or hide it and keep only the status. Admins set the default; each person can change it.

Colour legend

A "Colours" button on every board explains what each colour means, and a legend panel in each issue works even without the extension.

Adoption count

See how many people used the extension in the last 5 weeks, never their names, and send the others an invite in one click.

Set up in three steps

  1. Install Tintboard for Jira from the Atlassian Marketplace.
  2. Choose colours in Settings → Apps → Tintboard.
  3. Install the extension, or let IT push it to everyone.
