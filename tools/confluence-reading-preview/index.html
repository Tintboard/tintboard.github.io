Confluence reading comfort preview: font size, line spacing and page width

Long Confluence pages are tiring when lines run too long or the text is too small. Onboarding: your first week

Owned by the People team · 6 min read

Welcome aboard. This page walks you through your first week: the tools you'll use every day, the people to meet, and the few things that need doing before Friday. Read it once from top to bottom, then come back to it whenever you need a reminder.

On Monday, your manager will set up your accounts and add you to the team's spaces in Jira and Confluence. Take the afternoon to read the team handbook and the last two sprint reviews; they explain how we plan, what we shipped recently and what's coming next.

By Wednesday, pick up a small first task from the backlog with your onboarding buddy. Nobody expects it to be finished quickly: the goal is to walk through our whole flow once, from the ticket to the review to the release notes.

The preview needs JavaScript.

What makes a page comfortable to read

  • Line length: about 45 to 80 characters per line. WCAG's "Visual presentation" criterion (1.4.8) asks that blocks of text can be shown at no more than 80. Longer lines make readers lose their place on the way back.
  • Line spacing: at least 1.5 for body text. WCAG 1.4.12 checks that content still works at 1.5, and many readers with dyslexia find more spacing easier.
  • Text size: 16 px is a sensible minimum for long pages; larger text helps on big monitors and for low-vision readers.

What Confluence Cloud lets you change

Confluence Cloud has no font size or line spacing setting. A page's author can switch it between fixed and full width, and each reader can zoom the browser, which enlarges everything, menus included. More in how to change the font size in Confluence Cloud.

Set it for everyone

Tintboard for Confluence applies these settings to every page and blog post: a text size from 14 to 20 px, line spacing from 1.4 to 2.0 and a reading width, for the whole site or one space, and each reader can still pick their own. Free for up to 10 users.
