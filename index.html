Questions

Why is a browser extension needed?

Atlassian apps run in a sandbox and can't restyle Jira's or Confluence's own pages. The extension applies the colours your admin chose, in each person's browser. Without it, people still see the colour legend in each Jira issue.

Does any of our data leave Atlassian?

No. There is no Tintboard server. Rules are stored in your own site, signed so nobody can fake them, and the extension talks only to your atlassian.net site with your own session.

Does it work on team-managed boards?

Yes, including card colours, which Jira itself doesn't offer there.

Does it slow Jira down?

Labels and statuses are coloured within about 15 ms of Jira showing them. Card rules need one Jira search per 100 cards on screen.

What if Atlassian changes a page?

That colour switches itself off instead of breaking the page, and we fix it within 2 business days.

Can we add our own CSS to Confluence Cloud?

Not free CSS, on purpose: one wrong rule can hide content. Tintboard's page styles cover what teams ask for: headings in your colour, serif fonts, links always underlined and striped tables, for the site or each space.

Does it work on mobile?

No. Browser extensions don't run in Atlassian's mobile apps.

What happens if our licence lapses?

The colours switch off for everyone. Your rules are kept and come back as soon as the licence is renewed.
