Tintboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy covers the Tintboard apps for Jira Cloud and Confluence Cloud (the "Forge apps"), the Tintboard browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox (the "Extension"), and our website and support channels.

Who we are: Fares Baatour, micro-entrepreneur, 127 rue des Enfants du Paradis, 92100 Boulogne-Billancourt, France, France ("we"). Contact: hello@tintboard.com.

1. In short

  • Your Jira and Confluence content never leaves Atlassian. The Forge apps run on Atlassian's infrastructure and keep their settings in Atlassian's Forge storage and in your own Jira and Confluence. We operate no server that receives your data.
  • The Extension talks only to your own Atlassian site (*.atlassian.net). It reads the rules your admin configured, changes how pages look in your browser, and marks the weeks you used it on your own Jira profile. It sends nothing to us or to any third party.
  • We see personal data only when you contact us or when Atlassian shares licensing contact details with us as the app vendor.

We cannot read Forge storage ourselves outside the app's own code. The data is processed under Atlassian's infrastructure and security controls (Atlassian Trust Center). For this data, your organisation is the controller.

3. Data processed by the Extension

  • Personal preferences (on/off, font size, line height, reading width) are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local or its Firefox equivalent). They are not synced through us.
  • Page content: the Extension reads the Jira or Confluence page currently displayed only to apply styles. Issue fields it reads for card rules are kept in the page's memory for at most one minute and never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Requests: the Extension calls your own Atlassian site's APIs with your existing session to read the configured rules, the fields that card rules test (summary, labels, status, priority, type, assignee, due date, last-updated date, parent issue and components) and, once a day, to mark the current week on your Jira profile. It requests access to *.atlassian.net only.
  • Local storage: besides your preferences, the Extension keeps, per site, the state it last showed in the toolbar and the date it last marked your profile.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising. The Extension contains no third-party scripts and loads no remote code.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with:

  • Atlassian, which runs the Marketplace, billing and the Forge platform.
  • Our providers: OVHcloud (Zimbra), France for email, and GitHub, Inc. for website hosting. Each is bound by data processing terms.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case the transfer relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing. Write to hello@tintboard.com. We answer within one month.

For data stored in your Atlassian site (Section 2), contact your organisation's Jira or Confluence admin, who controls it. Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage. The tintboard.published and tintboardseen properties stay in your site until an admin deletes them through Atlassian's REST API; our documentation gives the exact calls.

You can complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

7. Security

  • No vendor-hosted infrastructure holds customer data.
  • The Extension uses Manifest V3, minimal permissions and no remote code.
  • Dependencies are scanned automatically.
  • Vulnerabilities can be reported to hello@tintboard.com.

8. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date at the top. Material changes are announced in the app's release notes.
